# Chatmu Studio — Edit the sound profile
**Version:** 1.0
**Requires:** Chatmu Desktop bridge; a `Sound/` folder in the artist's linked workspace folder.
**Related:** `chatmu-studio-sound-profile-build` (creates it), `chatmu-studio-sound-profile-produce` (uses it).

## What SOUND.md is

The artist's own description of how their project sounds and works, in their words. Chatmu
reads it before producing with their material and follows it over the numbers in
`profile.json`. The artist is the author: Chatmu proposes, the artist decides.

## HARD RULES

1. **Read before writing.** `local_read_file` on `<artist folder>/Sound/SOUND.md` (the hidden
   `[proyecto local]` block gives the folder). Write the WHOLE file back with
   `local_write_file`, changing only what was asked; keep everything else byte for byte
   (their wording, order, typos included).
2. **Show the change** in one or two lines before writing ("Agrego en Reglas → Hacer: …").
   For more than a small addition, show the new section and wait for "sí".
3. **Never touch `profile.json` by hand** (it is regenerated by the build) and never anything
   in `Sound/local/`.
4. One rule per line, concrete: what, where, how ("Waves CLA-76 on lead vocals, fast attack,
   4:1"), not adjectives.
5. Every edit adds a line under `## Changelog`: `- YYYY-MM-DD: <what changed>`.

## Where each kind of change goes

| The artist says | Section |
|---|---|
| "uso X en Y", "X siempre en el máster" | `## Effect chains (what I use and where)` (and a Do rule if it's always) |
| "nunca…", "no me gusta…" | `## Rules` → Don't |
| "siempre…", "trabajo a…" | `## Rules` → Do |
| BPM / key / meter ranges | `## Tempo, key and meter` |
| genres, references, mood, story | `## Identity` |
| samples or packs they love / avoid | `## Samples and packs` (+ a rule) |
| loudness / tone of their masters | `## Master` |
| a kept or discarded sketch and why | the matching rule + the Changelog line |

If a section is missing, add it with that heading, after the closest one.

## After a rebuild (SOUND.draft.md exists)

1. Read both files. List only the differences that matter (new plug-ins, a moved BPM range,
   new packs, new master numbers), max 6 bullets.
2. Ask which to apply. Apply them to SOUND.md (rules 1–5). Never replace their Identity,
   Rules or wording with the draft's.
3. Then the draft is no longer needed: tell the user they can delete `SOUND.draft.md`
   (don't delete it yourself unless they ask).

## No SOUND.md yet

Offer `chatmu-studio-sound-profile-build` (it learns from their sets and masters). If the
artist prefers to write it by hand, create `Sound/SOUND.md` with the sections above
(Identity, Tempo/key/meter, Song structure, Track template, Instruments and sounds, Effect
chains, Master, Samples and packs, Rules, Local assets, Changelog) and only what they said.
